# Signal study — what the trend score actually predicts

Generated by `scripts/signal_study.py` over cached public BTCUSD 5m candles
(`btc_trend_engine/research/information_coefficient.py`). No trade records,
no option chain, no credentials: features, forward returns, and forward
excursion only.

**Sample:** 115,188 candles, 2025-06-24 → 2026-07-29 (≈13 months), **114,419
scored decision bars**. The study replays the *production* scorer, so what is
measured is what the engine computes. Derivatives history (mark, index,
funding, open interest — 10,440 hourly bars each) is reconstructed so
`derivatives_context` can be scored for the first time.

Re-run 2026-07-29 against the current eight-component score and the ±40/±30
zone bands. The previous edition measured a six-component score on ±35/±15
and is superseded throughout.

## Headline

**Seven of the eight components have negative information coefficients at
every horizon the strategy trades. The one exception is the smallest weight
and the only non-price input.**

| component | weight | IC @30m | IC @60m | IC @120m | IC @240m |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `higher_timeframe_trend` | 0.30 | −0.017 | −0.025\* | −0.029 | −0.033 |
| `market_structure` | 0.18 | −0.028\* | −0.036\* | −0.036\* | −0.032 |
| `rsi_momentum` | 0.14 | −0.040\* | −0.043\* | −0.045\* | −0.037 |
| `lower_timeframe_momentum` | 0.13 | −0.044\* | −0.043\* | −0.043\* | −0.027 |
| `adx_trend_strength` | 0.10 | −0.016 | −0.015 | −0.014 | −0.006 |
| `breakout_quality` | 0.08 | −0.024\* | −0.021 | −0.019\* | −0.011 |
| **`derivatives_context`** | **0.07** | **+0.009** | **+0.010** | **+0.017** | **+0.027** |
| `order_flow` | 0.00 | — not computed — |
| **`trend_score`** | — | **−0.034\*** | **−0.039\*** | **−0.041\*** | −0.033 |

\* |t| > 2 on non-overlapping samples.

Three things changed materially from the previous edition:

**1. The composite is now significantly negative, not merely un-predictive.**
`trend_score` reaches t = −4.4 at 30m on 19,070 independent samples, against
−1.5 on the older, smaller sample. The finding is no longer "no demonstrated
edge"; it is "reliably pointed the wrong way over this period."

**2. `derivatives_context` is the only component with a positive IC**, and it
grows with horizon (+0.009 → +0.027). It is not statistically significant
(t = +1.3 at best), so this is a lead and not a result. But it is the only
non-price input in the model, it carries the smallest weight, and it is the
only one whose sign points the right way. Measured here for the first time.

**3. `adx_trend_strength` is no longer significant** (t = −1.5 … −0.5). An
earlier run on a 40k-bar sample showed it significant at all four horizons;
on 114k bars with `CALM_ADX_MAX` retuned 35 → 30, it is not. The earlier
reading did not survive more data.

## Component correlation — the score counts one opinion several times

| pair | Spearman |
|---|---|
| `lower_timeframe_momentum` ↔ `rsi_momentum` | **+0.778** |
| `market_structure` ↔ `rsi_momentum` | +0.694 |
| `higher_timeframe_trend` ↔ `rsi_momentum` | +0.675 |
| `rsi_momentum` ↔ `adx_trend_strength` | +0.672 |
| `lower_timeframe_momentum` ↔ `adx_trend_strength` | +0.583 |
| `higher_timeframe_trend` ↔ `market_structure` | +0.488 |
| `lower_timeframe_momentum` ↔ `breakout_quality` | +0.462 |
| … | … |
| `higher_timeframe_trend` ↔ `derivatives_context` | +0.267 |
| `rsi_momentum` ↔ `derivatives_context` | +0.154 |
| `breakout_quality` ↔ `derivatives_context` | +0.029 |

**This reverses the previous edition's conclusion.** That edition, measuring
six components, found overlaps of 0.36–0.46 and concluded collinearity was
*not* why the score failed. With `rsi_momentum` added that no longer holds:
it correlates **+0.778** with `lower_timeframe_momentum` and above +0.67 with
three others. Its 14% of weight bought very little independent information —
it mostly amplified a view the model already held, and held negatively.

`derivatives_context` is the mirror image: its highest correlation with any
price component is +0.267, and against most it is under +0.15. It is the only
part of the model saying something the rest is not.

## Calibration at the live zone boundaries

`hit_rate` is signed by the band, so 0.50 is a coin toss in either direction.

| band | zone it triggers | n | hit @30m | hit @60m | hit @120m | hit @240m |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| −100…−40 | `PE_2_ITM` (buy puts) | 24,524 | 0.466 | 0.462 | **0.452** | 0.468 |
| −40…−30 | HOLD | 8,215 | 0.488 | 0.480 | 0.475 | 0.481 |
| −30…+30 | `SHORT_MOVE` | 49,457 | 0.503 | 0.504 | 0.501 | 0.503 |
| +30…+40 | HOLD | 8,220 | 0.490 | 0.482 | 0.473 | 0.476 |
| +40…+100 | `CE_2_ITM` (buy calls) | 24,003 | **0.461** | **0.453** | 0.461 | 0.463 |

**Both directional entry bands sit below 0.50 at every horizon**, and the two
strongest bands are the worst cells in the table. The neutral band — which
takes no directional view — is the only one at 0.50, which is exactly what a
directionally uninformative signal should produce.

Widening the entry threshold from ±35 to ±40 did not fix this: the +40…+100
band is no better than the old +35…+100 band was.

## The sideways gate — does it select calm markets?

`SHORT_MOVE` does not trade direction. It sells an ATM straddle, is
indifferent to which way price goes, and is hurt by the largest move against
it at any point in the window. So the `-30..+30` row above is not evidence
about this zone: a band can post a perfect 0.50 directional hit rate while
moving 3% every time.

This section uses **forward max excursion** — `max(|price − entry|) / entry`
over the path, not the endpoint. Stages are cumulative and in the order the
live engine applies them.

| stage | n | share | 30m median | vs all | 60m median | vs all | 240m median | vs all |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| all scored bars | 114,419 | 100.0% | 0.257% | 1.00 | 0.370% | 1.00 | 0.775% | 1.00 |
| `\|score\| <= 30` | 49,533 | 43.3% | 0.235% | 0.92 | 0.342% | 0.92 | 0.734% | 0.95 |
| + 5m ADX < 30 | 44,279 | 38.7% | 0.230% | 0.89 | 0.334% | 0.90 | 0.723% | 0.93 |
| + regime safe | 41,181 | 36.0% | 0.231% | 0.90 | 0.337% | 0.91 | 0.726% | 0.94 |

**The gate works, weakly.** It selects markets about 10% calmer than average.
That is a real effect, and it is the only part of the model that measurably
does what it claims.

**Most of it is the score band** (1.00 → 0.92). The ADX calm test — which
replaced the retired 30-minute confirmation window on 2026-07-29 — adds
another 2–3 points while discarding 5,254 bars. That is a reasonable trade.

**The regime veto still contributes nothing and remains slightly negative.**
It removes 3,098 bars and the survivors are marginally *more* volatile at
every horizon (30m 0.230% → 0.231%, 60m 0.334% → 0.337%, 240m 0.723% →
0.726%). This is the third consecutive edition to find this. The likely cause
is volatility mean reversion: bars flagged `HIGH_VOL_SHOCK` have already had
their move, so vetoing them discards some of the calmest forward windows on
the tape.

## Stability

**Not yet re-run for this edition.** The two-half split below is from the
superseded sample (63,351 candles, six components, ±35/±15 bands) and is
retained only because it answers a question this edition does not:

| half | period | score IC @120m | CE band hit | PE band hit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| first | 2025-12-17 → 2026-04-16 | −0.039 | 0.455 | 0.469 |
| second | 2026-04-06 → 2026-07-25 | −0.041 | 0.442 | 0.471 |

Two disjoint periods, the same sign and nearly the same magnitude, so the
negative direction was not a single-regime artefact *for that
configuration*. Whether it holds for the current eight-component score on
±40/±30 is untested. Treat this table as suggestive of the new result, not
evidence for it.

The full-sample significance in this edition is much stronger than anything
the older split could show (composite t = −4.4 on 19,070 independent samples
against −1.5 before), but statistical significance over one continuous window
is not the same property as stability across disjoint windows. Re-running the
split is the outstanding item.

## What this does and does not establish

**Established.** The directional signal has no positive edge at 30m–4h, and
on this sample it is significantly negative (composite t = −4.4 at 30m).
Both directional entry bands are below a coin toss at every horizon. This
explains the walk-forward result — 1 of 4 folds positive out-of-sample,
pooled CI spanning zero — without needing to invoke bad luck.

**Established.** The sideways gate selects markets ~10% calmer than average,
the ADX calm test contributes 2–3 points of that, and the regime veto
contributes nothing.

**Not established.** That the signal is reliably *contrarian* and should be
inverted. **Do not flip the sign.** An IC of −0.04 is economically trivial
against the ~3% of premium a round trip costs in fees at 0–1 DTE (see the
fee-drag table in the project review), so even a real reversal effect of this
size would not survive execution. Inverting a losing signal is also the
classic way to convert one overfit into another.

**Not established.** That `derivatives_context` is predictive. Its IC is
positive at every horizon and rises with horizon, which is suggestive, but
t ≤ +1.3 is not significance. It is the best lead in the table, not a result.

**Not established.** That the sideways gate is enough to make selling the
straddle profitable. Movement is one half; what the straddle was *paid* is
the other, and nothing here measures it.

## Limitations

- `order_flow` (20% by design, 0.00 in `V1_WEIGHTS`) is still not computed.
- Forward returns **and forward excursion** are measured on the
  **perpetual**, not on the options actually traded
  ([ADR 0005](adr/0005-backtest-scope.md)). A signal weak on the underlying
  cannot be rescued by the option wrapper, but option P&L additionally
  depends on vol and theta. For `SHORT_MOVE` this means the study measures
  how far the underlying travelled, never what the straddle was paid — so it
  can rule a setup out, and cannot rule one in.
- IC measures monotone association only. A component could be informative
  conditionally (e.g. only in one regime) and score ~0 here. `Observation`
  carries `regime`, so this is testable; no conditional IC has been run.
- The sideways-gate and calibration figures are descriptive: samples overlap
  heavily (bars 5 minutes apart, horizons to 4 hours) and no significance
  test is attached to them. Only the IC table's `t` column is corrected for
  overlap, by subsampling.
- `derivatives_context` is scored here with `funding_percentile` included.
  Production omits it; `--as-running` reproduces that variant.

## Reproducing

```bash
python scripts/backtest.py fetch --days 400
python scripts/signal_study.py --derivatives --json signal_study.json
```

The gate and calibration sections come from the same run.
`--component-ceilings 50,35,25` (the default) sets the research ceilings
profiled; `--as-running` omits `funding_percentile` to match what production
actually computes.
