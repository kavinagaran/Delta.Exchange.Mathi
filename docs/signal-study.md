# Signal study — what the trend score actually predicts

Generated by `scripts/signal_study.py` over cached public BTCUSD 5m candles
(`btc_trend_engine/research/information_coefficient.py`). No trade records,
no option chain, no credentials: features, forward returns, and forward
excursion only.

**Sample:** 63,351 candles, 2025-12-17 → 2026-07-25 (≈7 months), 27k–31k
scored decision bars per half. The study replays the *production* scorer, so
what is measured is what the engine computes.

## Headline

**No component shows positive predictive power at any horizon the strategy
trades, and the composite score's strongest readings are its worst.**

This covers the score's **directional** claim, which is what the CE and PE
zones trade. The `SHORT_MOVE` zone asks a different question and is measured
separately in [the sideways gate](#the-sideways-gate--does-it-select-calm-markets)
below — where the answer is more favourable.

| component | weight | IC @30m | IC @60m | IC @120m | IC @240m |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `higher_timeframe_trend` | **0.40** | +0.001 | −0.003 | −0.003 | +0.003 |
| `market_structure` | 0.20 | −0.032 | −0.040 | −0.035 | +0.004 |
| `lower_timeframe_momentum` | 0.20 | −0.049\* | −0.064\* | −0.077 | −0.084 |
| `order_flow` | 0.00 | — not computed — |
| `breakout_quality` | 0.10 | −0.042 | −0.049 | −0.056 | −0.057 |
| `derivatives_context` | 0.10 | — not evaluated (no ticker history) — |
| **`trend_score`** | — | **−0.033** | **−0.043** | **−0.045** | **−0.032** |

\* |t| > 2 on non-overlapping samples.

The component carrying **40% of the weight has an IC of essentially zero at
every horizon** — it is neither right nor wrong, it is uninformative about
the next 30 minutes to 4 hours.

## Calibration at the live zone boundaries (120m horizon)

| score band | zone it triggers | n | hit rate | mean return |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| −100…−35 | `PE_2_ITM` (buy puts) | 6,405 | **0.475** | −0.012% |
| −35…−15 | HOLD | 4,063 | 0.491 | −0.014% |
| −15…+15 | `SHORT_MOVE` | 6,486 | 0.512 | +0.008% |
| +15…+35 | HOLD | 4,452 | 0.490 | −0.011% |
| +35…+100 | `CE_2_ITM` (buy calls) | 5,665 | **0.435** | −0.056% |

`hit_rate` is signed by the band, so 0.50 is a coin toss in either
direction. **Both directional entry zones sit below 0.50**, and the
strongest bullish band — where the engine buys 2-step ITM calls — is the
worst cell in the table.

## The sideways gate — does it select calm markets?

Everything above measures **direction**. `SHORT_MOVE` does not trade
direction: it sells an ATM straddle, is indifferent to which way price goes,
and is hurt by the largest move against it at any point in the holding
window. The `-15..+15` row of the calibration table above is therefore not
evidence about this zone at all — a band can post a flawless 0.50 directional
hit rate while moving 3% every time, and a signed return cannot see it.

So this section uses a different outcome: **forward max excursion**,
`max(|price − entry|) / entry` over the path, not the endpoint. Same cached
history, all 60,422 scored bars, no option data involved.

The stages below are cumulative and in the order the live engine applies
them, so each row shows what that condition added on top of the one above.

| stage | n | share | 30m median | vs all | 30m p90 | vs all | 60m median | vs all | 60m p90 | vs all |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| all scored bars | 60,422 | 100.0% | 0.276% | 1.00 | 0.727% | 1.00 | 0.400% | 1.00 | 1.040% | 1.00 |
| `\|score\| <= 15` | 14,633 | 24.2% | 0.250% | 0.90 | 0.627% | 0.86 | 0.359% | 0.90 | 0.917% | 0.88 |
| + 30m confirmation | 6,460 | 10.7% | 0.245% | 0.89 | 0.612% | 0.84 | 0.349% | 0.87 | 0.897% | 0.86 |
| + regime safe | 5,820 | 9.6% | 0.247% | 0.89 | 0.622% | 0.86 | 0.354% | 0.88 | 0.922% | 0.89 |
| + `max\|component\| <= 50` | 3,624 | 6.0% | 0.235% | 0.85 | 0.617% | 0.85 | 0.342% | 0.86 | 0.936% | 0.90 |
| + `max\|component\| <= 35` | 2,027 | 3.4% | 0.235% | 0.85 | 0.622% | 0.86 | 0.340% | 0.85 | 0.993% | 0.95 |
| + `max\|component\| <= 25` | 1,077 | 1.8% | 0.225% | 0.82 | 0.604% | 0.83 | 0.336% | 0.84 | 0.959% | 0.92 |

**The gate works — weakly.** It selects markets roughly 10–15% calmer than
average on both the median and the p90 tail. That is a real effect and a
better result than the directional components manage.

**Almost all of it comes from the score band alone** (1.00 → 0.90). The
30-minute confirmation adds another 1–3% while discarding 56% of the
remaining bars.

**The regime veto contributes nothing, and is slightly negative.** It removes
640 bars and the survivors are marginally *more* volatile at every horizon
(30m 0.245% → 0.247%, 60m 0.349% → 0.354%, 120m 0.518% → 0.528%, 240m 0.764%
→ 0.776%). The veto exists to keep the engine out of volatility shocks, and
on this measure it does not do that. The likely reason is volatility
mean-reversion: bars flagged `HIGH_VOL_SHOCK` have already had their move, so
vetoing them discards some of the calmest forward windows on the tape. This
is the compression trap running in reverse, and it is the same reason a
"quietest range in N bars" filter would be a mistake here.

### The component-agreement ceiling

Added 2026-07-28 (`ZonePolicy.sideways_max_component_abs`). A score near zero
has two causes the weighted sum cannot distinguish: every component is quiet,
or large opposing components cancelled. `0.40 × (+50) + 0.20 × (−100)` is
also zero — a market whose timeframes violently disagree, scoring dead centre
of the neutral band. The ceiling bounds the widest single component instead
of their sum.

Relative median and p90 by horizon, against all bars:

| ceiling | share | 30m med | 60m med | 120m med | 240m med | 30m p90 | 60m p90 | 120m p90 | 240m p90 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| (gate only) | 9.6% | 0.89 | 0.88 | 0.91 | 0.91 | 0.86 | 0.89 | 0.92 | 0.88 |
| 50 | 6.0% | 0.85 | 0.86 | 0.89 | 0.92 | 0.85 | 0.90 | 0.94 | 0.85 |
| 35 | 3.4% | 0.85 | 0.85 | 0.90 | 0.95 | 0.86 | 0.95 | 0.97 | 0.91 |
| 25 | 1.8% | 0.82 | 0.84 | 0.89 | 0.94 | 0.83 | 0.92 | 0.93 | 0.89 |

Two limits on what this buys, both visible above:

1. **It is a 30–60m effect.** The median improvement is gone by 120m and
   reverses by 240m.
2. **It does not improve the tail.** The score band moves p90; the ceiling on
   top of it does not, and at 60m and 120m it makes p90 modestly *worse*. A
   short straddle does not die at the median.

**50 is the production value.** It captures effectively all the median
improvement that 35 does while leaving ~78% more bars eligible, and the
50/35/25 spread is inside the noise of an overlapping sample. It bounds a
failure mode; it is not an edge.

### What the sideways numbers do and do not establish

**Established.** The neutral band selects measurably calmer markets than
average, the confirmation window and the component ceiling add a little at
30–60m, and the regime veto adds nothing.

**Not established.** That any of this is enough to make selling the straddle
profitable. Movement is only one half — the other half is what the straddle
was paid, which nothing here measures. Nor are these differences
significance-tested: the samples overlap heavily (bars 5 minutes apart,
horizons to 4 hours), the figures are descriptive, and a 0.85 against a 0.89
could be noise.

## Stability

Split into two disjoint halves, the result is the same:

| half | period | score IC @120m | CE band hit | PE band hit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| first | 2025-12-17 → 2026-04-16 | −0.039 | 0.455 | 0.469 |
| second | 2026-04-06 → 2026-07-25 | −0.041 | 0.442 | 0.471 |

Two independent periods, ~7 months, the same sign and nearly the same
magnitude. This is not a single-regime artefact.

## Component correlation

| pair | Spearman |
|---|---|
| `lower_timeframe_momentum` ↔ `breakout_quality` | +0.455 |
| `higher_timeframe_trend` ↔ `market_structure` | +0.413 |
| `market_structure` ↔ `lower_timeframe_momentum` | +0.362 |
| `market_structure` ↔ `breakout_quality` | +0.204 |
| `higher_timeframe_trend` ↔ `lower_timeframe_momentum` | +0.064 |
| `higher_timeframe_trend` ↔ `breakout_quality` | +0.048 |

Moderate, not redundant. The prior suspicion that the four price components
were "one opinion counted four times" is **not** supported: at 0.36–0.46 they
overlap materially but each still carries distinct variation. Collinearity is
not the reason the score fails.

## What this does and does not establish

**Established.** The signal has no positive edge at 30m–4h. This is
consistent across two disjoint halves and explains the walk-forward result
(1 of 4 folds positive out-of-sample, pooled CI spanning zero) without
needing to invoke luck.

**Not established.** That the signal is reliably *contrarian*. Per-half
t-statistics on independent samples are −1.4 and −1.5, short of conventional
significance. **Do not flip the sign.** A −0.04 IC is also economically
tiny next to the ~3% of premium a round trip costs in fees at 0–1 DTE, so
even a real reversal effect of this size would not survive execution.

## Limitations

- `derivatives_context` (10%) was not evaluated — the study supplies no
  funding/OI/basis history. It remains untested, not cleared.
- `order_flow` (20% by design, 0.00 in `V1_WEIGHTS`) is not computed at all.
- Forward returns **and forward excursion** are measured on the
  **perpetual**, not on the options actually traded
  ([ADR 0005](adr/0005-backtest-scope.md)). A signal weak on the underlying
  cannot be rescued by the option wrapper, but option P&L additionally
  depends on vol and theta. For `SHORT_MOVE` specifically this means the
  study measures how far the underlying travelled, never what the straddle
  was paid — so it can rule a setup out, and cannot rule one in.
- IC measures monotone association only. A component could be informative in
  a non-monotone or conditional way (e.g. only in a particular regime) and
  score ~0 here. `Observation` now carries `regime`, so this is testable, but
  no conditional IC has been run yet.
- The sideways-gate figures are descriptive: overlapping samples, no
  significance test attached.

## Reproducing

```bash
python scripts/backtest.py fetch --days 400
python scripts/signal_study.py --json signal_study.json
```

The sideways-gate section comes from the same run.
`--component-ceilings 50,35,25` (the default) sets the ceilings profiled;
`--derivatives` additionally reconstructs funding/OI/basis so
`derivatives_context` can be scored, with `--as-running` to omit
`funding_percentile` and match what production actually computes.
