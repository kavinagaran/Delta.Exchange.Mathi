# Signal study — what the trend score actually predicts

Generated by `scripts/signal_study.py` over cached public BTCUSD 5m candles
(`btc_trend_engine/research/information_coefficient.py`). No trade records,
no option chain, no credentials: features and forward returns only.

**Sample:** 63,351 candles, 2025-12-17 → 2026-07-25 (≈7 months), 27k–31k
scored decision bars per half. The study replays the *production* scorer, so
what is measured is what the engine computes.

## Headline

**No component shows positive predictive power at any horizon the strategy
trades, and the composite score's strongest readings are its worst.**

| component | weight | IC @30m | IC @60m | IC @120m | IC @240m |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `higher_timeframe_trend` | **0.40** | +0.001 | −0.003 | −0.003 | +0.003 |
| `market_structure` | 0.20 | −0.032 | −0.040 | −0.035 | +0.004 |
| `lower_timeframe_momentum` | 0.20 | −0.049\* | −0.064\* | −0.077 | −0.084 |
| `order_flow` | 0.00 | — not computed — |
| `breakout_quality` | 0.10 | −0.042 | −0.049 | −0.056 | −0.057 |
| `derivatives_context` | 0.10 | — not evaluated (no ticker history) — |
| **`trend_score`** | — | **−0.033** | **−0.043** | **−0.045** | **−0.032** |

\* |t| > 2 on non-overlapping samples.

The component carrying **40% of the weight has an IC of essentially zero at
every horizon** — it is neither right nor wrong, it is uninformative about
the next 30 minutes to 4 hours.

## Calibration at the live zone boundaries (120m horizon)

| score band | zone it triggers | n | hit rate | mean return |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| −100…−35 | `PE_2_ITM` (buy puts) | 6,405 | **0.475** | −0.012% |
| −35…−15 | HOLD | 4,063 | 0.491 | −0.014% |
| −15…+15 | `SHORT_MOVE` | 6,486 | 0.512 | +0.008% |
| +15…+35 | HOLD | 4,452 | 0.490 | −0.011% |
| +35…+100 | `CE_2_ITM` (buy calls) | 5,665 | **0.435** | −0.056% |

`hit_rate` is signed by the band, so 0.50 is a coin toss in either
direction. **Both directional entry zones sit below 0.50**, and the
strongest bullish band — where the engine buys 2-step ITM calls — is the
worst cell in the table.

## Stability

Split into two disjoint halves, the result is the same:

| half | period | score IC @120m | CE band hit | PE band hit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| first | 2025-12-17 → 2026-04-16 | −0.039 | 0.455 | 0.469 |
| second | 2026-04-06 → 2026-07-25 | −0.041 | 0.442 | 0.471 |

Two independent periods, ~7 months, the same sign and nearly the same
magnitude. This is not a single-regime artefact.

## Component correlation

| pair | Spearman |
|---|---|
| `lower_timeframe_momentum` ↔ `breakout_quality` | +0.455 |
| `higher_timeframe_trend` ↔ `market_structure` | +0.413 |
| `market_structure` ↔ `lower_timeframe_momentum` | +0.362 |
| `market_structure` ↔ `breakout_quality` | +0.204 |
| `higher_timeframe_trend` ↔ `lower_timeframe_momentum` | +0.064 |
| `higher_timeframe_trend` ↔ `breakout_quality` | +0.048 |

Moderate, not redundant. The prior suspicion that the four price components
were "one opinion counted four times" is **not** supported: at 0.36–0.46 they
overlap materially but each still carries distinct variation. Collinearity is
not the reason the score fails.

## What this does and does not establish

**Established.** The signal has no positive edge at 30m–4h. This is
consistent across two disjoint halves and explains the walk-forward result
(1 of 4 folds positive out-of-sample, pooled CI spanning zero) without
needing to invoke luck.

**Not established.** That the signal is reliably *contrarian*. Per-half
t-statistics on independent samples are −1.4 and −1.5, short of conventional
significance. **Do not flip the sign.** A −0.04 IC is also economically
tiny next to the ~3% of premium a round trip costs in fees at 0–1 DTE, so
even a real reversal effect of this size would not survive execution.

## Limitations

- `derivatives_context` (10%) was not evaluated — the study supplies no
  funding/OI/basis history. It remains untested, not cleared.
- `order_flow` (20% by design, 0.00 in `V1_WEIGHTS`) is not computed at all.
- Forward returns are measured on the **perpetual**, not on the options
  actually traded ([ADR 0005](adr/0005-backtest-scope.md)). A signal weak on
  the underlying cannot be rescued by the option wrapper, but option P&L
  additionally depends on vol and theta.
- IC measures monotone association only. A component could be informative in
  a non-monotone or conditional way (e.g. only in a particular regime) and
  score ~0 here.

## Reproducing

python scripts/backtest.py fetch --days 400
